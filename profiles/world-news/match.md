# World news profile

Use this profile for current affairs and hard news: politics, government, policy, diplomacy, security, society, economy, and major events.

Typical items include breaking news, official statements, policy changes, elections, conflicts, protests, court rulings, corporate or economic developments of public significance, and reported analysis tied to a recent event.

Regions of interest include Hong Kong, mainland China, Taiwan, the United States, and international affairs generally.

Do not use this profile for pure technology or AI research releases (use `tech-news`), long-form technical arguments, tutorials, or promotional material without substantive information.
