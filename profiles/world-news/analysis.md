# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of current-affairs news for a reader who follows Hong Kong, mainland China, Taiwan, the United States, and international politics, policy, security, and society.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Major.** Events with wide, lasting consequences: major policy shifts, high-level political decisions, armed conflict developments, landmark rulings, election outcomes, economy-moving decisions, or state-level actions affecting many people.
- **7-8: High value.** Significant developments worth prompt attention: new policy proposals, official investigations, notable diplomatic moves, large-scale social or economic events, credible investigative reporting.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental political or social developments, regional matters, follow-ups worth knowing but not urgent.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine announcements, reposted wire copy with little new information, or content dominated by promotion or opinion without evidence.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, off-topic material, listicles, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Weigh factual specificity, the significance of the actors and decisions involved, and the breadth of impact. Prefer concrete reported facts (who, what, where, when, numbers) over vague commentary. When covering geopolitical disputes, present the reported event without adopting any party's framing. Do not reward sensational headlines; distinguish an announced intention from a completed action.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
